QRX (https://qrx.codes) makes QR codes that are also pictures. This app is a free companion to the QRX web service: people sign in with their QRX account and make, keep and share codes.

SIGN IN
Tap "Sign in with QRX" and sign in with the demo account below (email and password). The demo account has full access to every feature. If the sign-in page ever asks for an emailed code, use "Use an API key" at the bottom of the first screen instead, with the API key at the end of this note.

PURCHASES
The app has no in-app purchases and sells nothing. It shows no prices and has no buttons, links or wording that lead to buying, in any storefront. Every account gets a daily allowance of codes, shown on the Account tab. Some people also pay for QRX on our website for more; the app lets them sign in and use their account as a free stand-alone companion to a paid web-based tool (guideline 3.1.3(f)).

SIGN IN WITH APPLE
The QRX sign-in page offers Sign in with Apple next to Google and email (guideline 4.8).

TRY IT
1. Create tab: type a picture ("a paper boat on a calm lake at dawn, watercolour"), a link (example.com), pick a style, tap Create code.
2. A code takes about a minute. You can leave the screen; "Notify me when it's ready" turns on a local notification.
3. On the result: Share, Save (to Photos) and Copy link. "Report a problem with this image" sends a report to the QRX team without leaving the app.
4. My codes lists every code; Account shows today's allowance.

AI-GENERATED PICTURES
Pictures come from QRX's own image model, run on QRX's servers, from the person's prompt. Prompts pass an obscenity filter before anything is made, and offensive prompts are refused. Every picture can be reported in the app; reports go to the QRX team for review. Codes are private to the account: the app has no sharing between users, no chat and no public feed.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Account tab → Delete account → "Continue to delete account" opens the QRX account page (Security section), where "Delete account" removes the account and its codes immediately.
